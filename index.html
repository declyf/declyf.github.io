Questions

How do iPhone users install it without the App Store?

Open the site in Safari, tap “Open on iPhone”, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon and opens full screen, like any other app.

Can coaches work on a laptop?

Yes. The web app runs the whole of DECLYF in a browser, which is where most coaches build plans and review the week.

Why is Android a direct download?

So every improvement reaches people the same day instead of waiting on store review. A Play Store listing is planned.

Do clients pay anything?

No. The client app is free, including the plans their coach sends.

Where does the data live?

On the phone first, then backed up to a private database. Only the client and the coach they joined can see it, and a client can leave a coach at any time.
