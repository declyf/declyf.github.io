Questions coaches ask.

What does it cost?

Clients and solo trackers pay nothing. Coaches will pay monthly by client count, from a draft $5 for up to 5 clients. Coach plans are free during early access, and there are no payments in the app yet.

How do my clients join?

Share your coach code, or add them by email. They get a request in their Profile, accept it, and appear in your list. Until they accept, you cannot see any of their data.

What can I see for each client?

Their meals, workouts and weight, day by day. The list shows who is on track, slipping or needs a nudge, and each client has a 14 day view.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging works with no signal. Changes are saved on the phone and sync when it is back online.

Where does the data live?

On the phone first, then synced to a private database. Only the client and the coach they joined can see it, and a client can leave a coach at any time.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not in the App Store yet. On iPhone you add the web app to your home screen from Safari. On Android you install it from Chrome. A full Android app download is coming soon.

Can clients use it without a coach?

Yes. Pick solo at sign-up and track food, workouts and weight on your own, free.

Can I import a plan from a PDF?

Not yet. Plans are built inside DECLYF today.
