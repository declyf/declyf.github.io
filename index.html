Questions

Straight answers

How do iPhone users install it without the App Store?

Open this page in Safari, tap “Open DECLYF on iPhone”, then use Share → Add to Home Screen. It then behaves like any other app: its own icon, full screen, no browser bars.

Why is Android a direct download?

So every improvement reaches you the same day instead of waiting on store review. A Play Store listing is planned.

Do my clients pay anything?

No. The client app is free, including the plans you send them.

Where does my clients' data live?

On their phone first, then backed up to a private database. Only the client and the coach they joined can see it, and a client can leave a coach at any time.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Meals, workouts and weigh-ins all save on the phone and sync when the signal comes back.
